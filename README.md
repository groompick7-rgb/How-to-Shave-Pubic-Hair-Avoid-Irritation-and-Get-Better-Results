Learning How to Shave Pubic Hair safely starts with good preparation. First, trim longer hair with a clean body trimmer. This makes shaving easier and helps reduce pulling. Take a warm shower for a few minutes to soften the hair and skin.

Apply a gentle, fragrance-free shaving gel or cream. Using a clean, sharp razor, shave slowly in the direction of hair growth. Avoid pressing too hard, especially around sensitive areas. Rinse the razor often to keep the blades clear.

After shaving, rinse the skin with cool water and gently pat it dry. Apply a mild, fragrance-free moisturizer to reduce dryness. Avoid tight clothing and heavy fragrances immediately afterward.
